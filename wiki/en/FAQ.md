# FAQ

**Which radios?** All FrSky radios with Ethos and a colour display: 800 × 480, 640 × 360, 480 × 320 and 480 × 272. Tested on a real radio only on the X18RS.

**Does it need a vario sensor?** For vario, altitude and thermal detection yes. Without telemetry Glider shows "–".

**Release height stays "–"?** Check the launch type and its switch (tow release or motor off).

**Can I delete a flight?** Yes, each flight on its own (page 3 or the menu), with a question first.
