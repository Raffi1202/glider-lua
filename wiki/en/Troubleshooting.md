# Troubleshooting

- **"too much RAM" / not enough Lua memory:** all Lua scripts share one memory (about 2048 KB on the X18RS). Switch the radio off and on. Remove scripts you do not use.
- **Sensors missing:** discover the sensors in Ethos first, then **Find sensors (empty only)** in the widget menu. After deleting sensors in Ethos reload the model.
- **No sound on alarm:** "Sound and voice on alarm" off or volume 0. The mute switch lets red alarms through.
- **After an update:** restart the radio. Settings and flights stay.

More: manual chapter 9.
