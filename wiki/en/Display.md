# Display

Glider detects the layout by itself.

**Full screen:** three pages. Page 1 shows vario (bar, average 20 s, max), altitude (max, release height), flight time, the vario graph of the last 2 minutes with the thermal threshold and a bottom row with receiver battery, motor battery and link.

![Page 1, English](../../docs/bilder-simulator/Glider%20-%20Sim%20800x480%2012%20Englisch.png)

Page 2 shows the batteries (power), page 3 the flights with a summary and a list; each flight can be opened and deleted on its own.

**Compact view** (one frame with top and bottom bar): the key values with rings and fields.

![Compact view, English](../../docs/bilder-simulator/Glider%20-%20Sim%20472x210%2022%20Kompakt%20Englisch.png)

Status bar: alarm state, flight phase, motor state (E-glider: MOTOR OFF / MOTOR ON / MOTOR RUNNING), link and flight number.
The pages Power and Flights exist in full screen only. Details: manual chapter 5.
